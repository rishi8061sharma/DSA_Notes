=> 1) Retrieve the total revenue earned by each film category, 
but only include categories where the total revenue is greater than $1,000. 
Order the results by revenue in descending order.

Ans: 
SELECT cat.name AS category, SUM(pay.amount) AS total_revenue 
from category AS cat
JOIN film_category AS fc ON fc.category_id = cat.category_id
JOIN inventory AS inv ON  fc.film_id = inv.film_id
JOIN rental AS ren ON inv.inventory_id = ren.inventory_id
JOIN payment AS pay ON ren.rental_id = pay.rental_id
GROUP BY cat.name
HAVING SUM(pay.amount) > 1000



=> 2) List the customers who have made at least 30 rentals, 
and for each of these customers, display their customer ID and the count of rentals they've made


/*
List the customers who have made at least 30 rentals, and for each of these customers, 
display their customer ID and the count of rentals they've made
*/
USE sakila;

SELECT 
    cust.customer_id,
    COUNT(ren.rental_id) AS rental_count
FROM customer cust
JOIN rental ren 
    ON cust.customer_id = ren.customer_id
GROUP BY cust.customer_id
HAVING COUNT(ren.rental_id) >= 30;

/*OR*/ 

SELECT 
    customer_id,
    COUNT(*) AS rental_count
FROM rental
GROUP BY customer_id
HAVING COUNT(*) >= 30;



=> 3) Find rental_duration which is rental_duration of more than 200 movies

SELECT 
rental_duration,
COUNT(*) AS movie_count
FROM film f
GROUP BY f.rental_duration 
HAVING COUNT(*) > 200;
